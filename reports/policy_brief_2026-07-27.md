# PaperTrail Policy Brief — 2026-07-27

**Overview**
A total of 112 exam paper leak incidents have been tracked in India. The data shows a noticeable increase in incidents over the years, with a significant spike in 2021, 2022, and 2024. The overall trend indicates a growing concern regarding exam paper leaks in the country.

**State-wise Hotspots**
The data reveals that certain states are more prone to exam paper leaks. Madhya Pradesh, Uttar Pradesh, and Rajasthan are among the top states with the highest number of incidents, accounting for 13, 12, and 11 incidents, respectively. Other states, such as Uttarakhand, Bihar, and Haryana, also feature prominently in the list. The high incidence of exam paper leaks in these states may be attributed to various factors, including inadequate security measures and weaknesses in the examination system.

**Policy Impact: The 2024 Public Examinations Act**
The introduction of the 2024 Public Examinations Act appears to have coincided with a decrease in the total number of incidents, with 93 incidents reported before the Act and 19 incidents reported after. The arrests rate has shown a slight increase, from 55.9% to 57.9%, while the confirmed rate has decreased from 87.1% to 52.6%. However, it is essential to interpret these numbers with caution, as the post-Act period covers only approximately 2 years, compared to the 20-year pre-Act period. The decrease in the confirmed rate could reflect either a genuine reduction in verified leaks or an increase in incidents still under investigation.

**Outlook**
The forecast suggests a directional estimate of around 7.9 incidents in 2027 and 2028. This should be viewed as a rough indicator of the potential trend, rather than a precise prediction. The actual number of incidents may vary, and it is crucial to continue monitoring the situation to assess the effectiveness of the 2024 Public Examinations Act and identify areas for further improvement.
